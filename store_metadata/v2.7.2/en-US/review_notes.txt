ARchScan works without an account, login, subscription, advertising, or in-app purchases. All projects are stored locally on the device.

Camera access is required only while measuring. On supported devices the app selects the shared AR scanner; otherwise it offers the Basic Scanner, where measurements are entered manually.

Suggested review:
1. Create a project and a room.
2. Grant camera permission.
3. Measure and close the room.
4. Open the 2D plan to edit walls and add doors or windows.
5. Export JSON, PDF, or 2D DXF through the system share sheet.
6. Open Privacy and Data to delete local projects.
